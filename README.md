## 🎮 About the Game

Crimson Moon: Deluxe Edition Upgrade is downloadable content for Crimson Moon, a gothic action RPG developed by ProbablyMonsters and released on September 1, 2026. The upgrade adds the Ceremonial Armor Set, the Axe of the Gods, and access to the planned post-launch expansion.

## 🔥 Project Overview

**This tool** is a comprehensive trainer for **Crimson Moon: Deluxe Edition Upgrade**. This trainer enhances Crimson Moon's mission-based combat and progression systems with customizable health, damage, resource, equipment, and angelic transformation options. It is intended for solo play and helps players experiment with builds, explore Gildenarch, and complete replayable missions with fewer restrictions.

**Target Audience:** Players who want to experiment with Nephilim builds, test weapons and armor, reduce repeated farming, or make difficult missions and boss encounters more accessible.

> **⚠️ IMPORTANT:** This tool is for **educational and private use only**. It does not modify any game files — all changes are temporary and reset when the game closes. The tool is **undetectable**, but **use it at your own risk**. Always back up your save files before use.

- **Damage Multiplier** — Adjusts outgoing damage to speed up encounters against enemies, elites, and bosses.

## 📖 Detailed Feature Guide

### Movement & Exploration

Move through Gildenarch's Wards normally and use the trainer's gameplay options only when needed. A protected health state is useful during exploration, while movement should remain manual so mission routes, secrets, and encounters can still be learned naturally.

### Tools & Inventory

Prepare weapons, armor, consumables, Boons, and an Angel before deploying from the hub. Use resource and reward options after collecting legitimate items, then visit the Blacksmith to forge or upgrade permanent equipment. Avoid changing inventory values while menus are loading or during co-op matchmaking.

### World & Environment

Begin each hunt at Sanctus Clypeus, select a mission at the War Table, and enter the chosen Ward. Missions contain enemies, elite encounters, secrets, loot, and bosses. Return to the Church between runs to manage equipment, speak with NPCs, unlock progression, and plan the next incursion.

### Combat & Weapons

Crimson Moon emphasizes melee combat, parries, finishers, supernatural powers, and angelic transformations. Use damage and defense controls conservatively for a smoother experience, or enable stronger settings when testing builds against difficult enemies and Dead God encounters.
